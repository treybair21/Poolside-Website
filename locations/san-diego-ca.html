The San Diego Market

Oceanside, Carlsbad, and Vista have some of the densest pockets of pool ownership in North County, with a mix of established 1980s-and-90s subdivisions and newer development further inland. Escondido and San Marcos offer similar density at a more affordable price point. Coastal neighborhoods closer to La Jolla and Del Mar carry higher home values, but accounts tend to be more spread out, which can mean more drive time per stop.

Why San Diego Works for Pool Routes

  • Mild, consistent climate. San Diego rarely sees the harsh winter drop-off that slows service demand in other parts of the country.
  • High pool density in North County. Oceanside, Carlsbad, and Vista pack a lot of pool-owning homes into a manageable service radius.
  • Established homeowner base. Many accounts have owned their homes for years and are used to paying for regular service, which supports low customer turnover.

What a San Diego Route Is Worth

San Diego follows the same national framework: 10 to 12 times monthly service revenue. Routes concentrated in a tight North County radius, with strong autopay enrollment and add-on services like filter cleaning, typically land toward the top of that range.
