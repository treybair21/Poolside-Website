Pool Routes for Sale in San Antonio, TX

Buy or sell a pool route in the San Antonio metro · Poolside Business Brokers

San Antonio doesn't get the same national attention as Austin, but for pool route buyers that's part of the appeal. Home prices are lower, competition for routes is a little thinner, and the summers are every bit as hot — which means the fundamentals for a service business are just as strong, often at a lower buy-in.

If you're weighing whether to buy a route here or you're ready to sell one you've built, we can walk you through what routes in this market are actually trading for right now.

The San Antonio Market

Stone Oak and Alamo Ranch have been the fastest-growing residential areas in the metro for a while, and both are full of newer homes built with pools. Boerne and Bulverde, just north of the city, have seen similar growth as San Antonio pushes into the Hill Country. Closer to downtown and on the South Side, pool ownership drops off, so most route density concentrates in the north and northwest parts of the metro.

Why San Antonio Works for Buyers

  • Lower entry cost. Routes here often price below comparable routes in Austin, simply because home values and service rates run a bit lower.
  • A long, reliable summer. Demand runs hot from May through September, similar to the rest of Central Texas.
  • Continued suburban growth. New construction to the north and northwest keeps adding pool-equipped homes.

What a San Antonio Route Is Worth

The same national pricing framework applies here: 10 to 12 times monthly service revenue. Routes concentrated in Stone Oak or Alamo Ranch, with strong autopay enrollment and low customer turnover, tend to land at the higher end of that range.

Buying a Pool Route in San Antonio

Because San Antonio is more affordable to enter, it's a common first purchase for buyers new to the industry. Ask for the same documentation you'd want anywhere: 12+ months of revenue records, a service map, and honest numbers on customer turnover. The seller should be willing to provide real training during the transition, not just a handoff.

Frequently Asked Questions: San Antonio Pool Routes

How much does a pool route sell for in San Antonio, TX?

Typically 10 to 12 times monthly service revenue. A $7,000/month route generally lists between $70,000 and $84,000.

Is San Antonio a good market for buying a pool route?

Yes — lower home prices and steady summer demand make it an accessible entry point, often more affordable than nearby Austin.

What parts of San Antonio have the best pool route density?

Stone Oak, Alamo Ranch, Boerne, and Bulverde have the newest, densest pool-equipped housing.

How do I sell my pool route in San Antonio?
