The Tampa Bay Market

Wesley Chapel, Land O'Lakes, and Riverview have led the metro's growth on the east and south sides, adding dense new subdivisions with pools as a standard feature. Brandon and Valrico offer a similar mix at a slightly more established stage. South Tampa and the coastal communities in Pinellas County (St. Petersburg, Clearwater) carry older, higher-value homes with established pools, though route density there varies more by neighborhood.

Why Tampa Works for Pool Routes

  • Consistent growth. New construction keeps adding pool-equipped homes across the metro's suburbs.
  • Long service season. Warm, humid weather keeps demand high from spring through fall, with year-round maintenance needs.
  • A mix of new and established neighborhoods gives buyers options between growth-focused routes and stable, mature ones.

What a Tampa Route Is Worth

Tampa follows the same national framework: 10 to 12 times monthly service revenue. Routes concentrated in a specific growth corridor, with strong autopay enrollment and low customer turnover, typically land toward the top of that range.

Frequently Asked Questions: Tampa Pool Routes

How much does a pool route sell for in Tampa, FL?

Typically 10 to 12 times monthly service revenue. A $9,000/month route generally lists between $90,000 and $108,000.

Wesley Chapel, Land O'Lakes, Riverview, and Brandon lead on new growth. South Tampa and Pinellas County carry established, higher-value homes.
