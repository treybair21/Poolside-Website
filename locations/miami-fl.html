Pool Routes for Sale in Miami, FL

Buy or sell a pool route in the Miami metro · Poolside Business Brokers

Miami's climate never really gives pool routes a slow season, and the concentration of single-family homes with pools in neighborhoods like Kendall and Pinecrest makes South Florida one of the highest-value markets in the country for this business.

If you're looking to buy a route in Miami or sell one you've built, the local details — salt air, hurricane season, condo vs. single-family housing stock — matter more here than in most markets, and we factor all of it into pricing.

The Miami Market

Kendall, Pinecrest, Palmetto Bay, and Coral Gables have some of the highest concentrations of single-family homes with pools in the metro, and property values there support strong monthly service rates. Doral and parts of West Miami-Dade have grown quickly with newer construction. It's worth noting that a lot of Miami's growth has been in condos and townhomes, which typically use HOA-managed community pools rather than individual residential accounts — so route density concentrates in the established single-family neighborhoods.

Why Miami Works for Pool Routes

  • Year-round demand. There's no real off-season for pool maintenance in South Florida's tropical climate.
  • Higher home values. Miami-area homes often support higher monthly service rates than the national average, which lifts route valuations.
  • Established, stable neighborhoods in Kendall, Pinecrest, and Coral Gables provide long-tenured customer bases.

What a Miami Route Is Worth

Miami routes generally price at the higher end of the national 10 to 12x monthly revenue range, reflecting the area's home values. Routes concentrated in Pinecrest or Coral Gables, with strong autopay enrollment and low turnover, often command premium pricing.

Buying a Pool Route in Miami

Salt air corrosion is a real factor here — inspect equipment and vehicles carefully, since coastal exposure accelerates wear compared to inland markets. Also ask about the seller's hurricane season track record: how missed service days were handled and whether customers stayed on through storm disruptions.

Frequently Asked Questions: Miami Pool Routes

How much does a pool route sell for in Miami, FL?

Typically 10 to 12 times monthly service revenue, often at the higher end given local home values. A $10,000/month route generally lists between $100,000 and $120,000.

Is Miami a good market for buying a pool route?

Yes — year-round demand and higher home values make it one of the strongest markets in the country, with salt air being the main added maintenance consideration.

What parts of Miami have the best pool route density?

Kendall, Pinecrest, Coral Gables, and Palmetto Bay lead on single-family pool density.

How do I sell my pool route in Miami?

Work with a broker who understands South Florida's housing mix and prices accordingly. We typically see qualified offers within about two weeks of listing.
