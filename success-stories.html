Orlando, FL

Ten Months on the Market Elsewhere. One Month With Us.

This owner had been trying to sell their route in the greater Orlando area for over ten months before we got involved. They'd even listed with another broker for a stretch, and it just never went anywhere. By the time they reached out to us, they were understandably a little skeptical that anything would change.

We got them connected with the right buyer within about a month, and the deal closed not long after. The buyer was a larger pool cleaning company looking to grow through acquisition — and instead of just handing over the keys and walking away, our seller ended up taking on a role with the company that bought them. What started as an exit turned into something closer to a career move.

Phoenix, AZ

20 Years In the Business, and He Didn't Want a Typical Broker

This one was a little different. The owner had been running his route in Phoenix for two decades, had five employees working under him, and was finally ready to retire. The catch: he'd had a bad experience with a broker in the past and wasn't interested in being formally represented again — no exclusive listing agreement, none of it.

Rather than walk away from the conversation, we found an arrangement that actually worked for him. We reached out to a buyer already in our network, and helped keep both sides moving toward closing without ever pushing him into a relationship he wasn't comfortable with. From our first conversation to closing day, the whole thing took about six weeks — and he got the retirement he was after, on his terms.
